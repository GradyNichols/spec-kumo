Kumo

Modern Japanese Ramen ⋅ Los Angeles, CA

Kumo was founded in 2016 by Kenji Nakamura, a Osaka-born chef who spent ... eight years working in traditional ramen-ya across Japan before relocating to Los Angeles with one goal — bringing authentic Japanese ramen technique to the California dining scene without sacrificing soul for style. What started as a 12-seat counter in Silver Lake quickly became one of LA's most talked-about ramen destinations, earning a devoted following for its meticulously crafted broths, which simmer for a minimum of 18 hours before a single bowl is served.
